# Visual Design & Trends 2026 (Priority 3)

For the public showcase (vitrine) and polished cockpit surfaces. Most-used and best-rated directions.

## Bento grids 🍱 — APPS TIER ONLY

- Modular, asymmetric card blocks (Japanese lunchbox). Adopted by **Apple, Samsung, Microsoft, Google**.
- Great for varied content without overwhelming.
- **In CFAO Academy, bento belongs in the future apps** (launched from the hub), NOT on the Accueil. The Accueil is the public front door and must stay **sober and homogeneous** (uniform grids) for bureaucrats and clients alike.

## Glassmorphism

- Translucent layers, soft blur, subtle shadows — pairs well with dark mode.
- **Restrict to navigation, modals, and panels.** Don't apply everywhere (readability cost).
- Project rule: hero text always inside a `.glass-panel`.

## Minimalism, evolved

- Minimalist structure **+** bold typographic expression **+** purposeful motion **+** rich micro-interactions.
- Calm, toned-down palettes; lots of white space.

## Typography as hero

- Oversized headlines, custom fonts, layered styles — typography does storytelling, not just legibility.
- One clear primary action per view; restrained color palette.

## Dark mode — NOT REQUIRED (client decision)

- The CFAO Academy client did **not** request dark mode. Ship a single, well-tuned **light** theme.
- (General reference, if ever revisited: build light AND dark together via tokens; in dark use slightly heavier weights + more leading; avoid pure black/white.)

## Human / anti-AI touch (optional accent)

- Hand-drawn doodles, scribbles, intentional "imperfection" as a counter to AI-perfect visuals. Use sparingly, on-brand.

## Other current accents

- Organic shapes, bold colors, subtle 3D, sustainable/lightweight practices.
