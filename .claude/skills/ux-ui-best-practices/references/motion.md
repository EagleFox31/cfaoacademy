# Motion & Micro-interactions (Priority 4)

2026 shift = **restraint**. Purposeful motion that guides attention and gives feedback — not theatrics.

## Principles

- **Clarity, speed, purpose.** Every animation earns its place: feedback, orientation, or hierarchy.
- Calm micro-interactions replace gamification; strategic motion replaces extravagant effects.
- Micro-interactions measurably **increase form completion, reduce abandonment, encourage exploration** — small feedback cues drive conversion lifts.

## Where micro-interactions pay off

- Button/state feedback (hover, active, loading, success/error).
- Form validation cues (inline, immediate, gentle).
- Transitions that preserve context (shared-element, list reorder).
- Scroll-driven reveals — sober, not flashy (e.g. the project's Netflix-style crossfade per section).

## Guardrails

- **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`** — provide reduced/no-motion variants.
- Keep durations short (typically ~150-300ms for UI feedback); ease naturally.
- Don't animate large layout shifts that hurt CLS/perceived performance.
- Motion should never block or delay a task on tool screens.

## Kinetic typography (use carefully)

- Bold/oversized headline motion can make a first impression on the vitrine.
- Avoid chaotic text effects — purposeful motion that supports the message only.
